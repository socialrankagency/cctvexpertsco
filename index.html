Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our moving service in Bahrain.

How do I book a mover in Bahrain?

Booking a mover is easy. Call or WhatsApp us at +92 335 1248010 with your name, pickup location, destination, and move date. Our team confirms your booking immediately and provides a free quote within 30 minutes.

How much does moving cost in Bahrain?

Our moving prices start from BHD 25 for small apartments. A standard 1-2 bedroom move is around BHD 75, a full house move starts at BHD 150, and villa/office moves are quoted based on size. All prices are transparent with no hidden charges.

Do you provide packing materials?

Yes. We provide high-quality boxes, bubble wrap, tape, and packing paper as part of our service. Our professional packers ensure every item — fragile or heavy — is packed safely for transport.

Is my move insured?

Yes, all our moves are fully insured. In the rare case of any damage during transit, we cover the cost. Your peace of mind is our priority.

Do you offer international moving from Bahrain?

Yes. We offer international moving services from Bahrain to GCC countries, Asia, Europe, and beyond. We handle packing, customs clearance, shipping, and delivery to your destination.

How quickly can your team arrive?

For booked moves, our team arrives at the scheduled time. For urgent requests, we can dispatch a team within 2-4 hours depending on your location and crew availability.

Can you move heavy or large furniture?

Absolutely. Our trained crew can disassemble, move, and reassemble heavy furniture including beds, wardrobes, dining tables, and large appliances. We use proper equipment to prevent damage.

Do you offer storage services?

Yes. We have secure storage facilities for short-term and long-term needs. Whether you need to store items for a few days or several months, we have climate-controlled units available.
